Watch `ReadestStats pull:` lines in the log. Revert autostart afterwards (open control port).

**Pitfalls:** (1) `plugins/readest.koplugin` is a SYMLINK; it pointed at a deleted worktree (`readest-feat-koplugin-localsend`) so the plugin silently did not load (no Readest menu) -> now points at `/Users/chrox/dev/readest/apps/readest.koplugin`; re-check after any worktree cleanup.
