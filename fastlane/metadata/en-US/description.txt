Readest is an ebook reader for people who read a lot, and who want their books, notes and reading position on whatever device is nearest.

Open EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, CBZ, TXT and Markdown in one library. Pull books from OPDS catalogs or from your own Calibre server. Read, annotate, listen, translate.

Made for iPhone and iPad
• CarPlay, so you can keep listening on the road with playback controls on the dashboard
• Download read-aloud voices per book and listen with no connection at all
• Add books from Files, Mail or any share sheet, and read them in place instead of copying them into a second library
• Open up to four books at once on iPad and arrange them how you like

Reading
• Paginated or scrolling, with custom fonts, themes, spacing and margins
• Vertical text for CJK books
• Contrast and refresh tuned for e-ink screens
• Full-text search inside a book

Your library
• Browse, search and download from OPDS catalogs and public libraries
• Connect to Calibre or Calibre-Web, covers and metadata included
• Organize with bookshelf groups you can rename

Notes
• Highlights, bookmarks and structured notes
• Copy a highlight together with a deep link back to the passage
• Export your annotations

Listening and languages
• Text-to-speech with downloadable offline voices
• Translate a sentence, a page, or an entire book
• Wikipedia and dictionary lookup without leaving the page

Sync
• Readest Cloud, or bring your own: iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, WebDAV, S3
• KOReader sync keeps your progress in step with your e-reader
• iPhone, iPad, Mac, all other platforms and the web

Readest is open source.
