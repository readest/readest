**Yomi** is a modern, cross-platform ebook reader built for book lovers who value focus, customizability, and a clean reading interface. Created to work seamlessly with Biblophile, Yomi is designed to work beautifully across macOS, Windows, Linux, Android, iOS, and the Web, keeping your library and reading progress seamlessly synced wherever you go.

---

## 🌟 What You Get Out of Yomi

Yomi is designed around the philosophy of **deep reading**. Here is how Yomi elevates your reading experience:

*   **Immersive & Distraction-Free:** Focus entirely on your book with a clean interface that recedes as you read. Customize colors, layouts, margins, and line heights to create your perfect reading environment.
*   **Always in Sync:** Never lose your page. Your books, reading progress, highlights, and notes automatically sync across your phone, tablet, laptop, and web browser.
*   **Active Learning Tools:** Look up terms instantly with integrated Wikipedia/dictionaries, translate passages with one click, or use built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) to listen when you're on the go.
*   **Designed for Study & Research:** Read two documents side-by-side using the parallel split-screen mode, customize tags, and organize your digital bookshelf your way.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

*   **Broad Format Support:** Read EPUB, PDF, MOBI, KF8 (AZW3), FB2, CBZ, TXT, and Markdown (MD) files.
*   **Highly Customizable Layouts:** Choose between paginated pages or continuous scrolling. Adjust fonts, line spacing, margins, and select beautiful themes (including full Dark Mode support).
*   **Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Read-Along:** Listen to books using system text-to-speech engine or enjoy EPUB 3 media overlays.
*   **Integrated Dictionaries & Translators:** Import dictionary packs (including Yomitan/RDICT) and use integrated DeepL or Yandex for instant translations.
*   **Parallel Reading:** Split-screen reading allows you to view two books or documents side-by-side.
*   **Koreader & Calibre Sync:** Fully integrates with Calibre/OPDS catalogs and synchronizes reading progress with Koreader devices.
*   **Accessibility First:** Fully navigable via keyboard shortcuts and optimized for screen readers like VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, and Orca.

## 📖 How to Use Yomi

### 1. Adding Books to Your Library
*   **Import Files:** Drag and drop your ebook files directly into the application window, or use the **Import** button in the dashboard to add files from your device.
*   **Online Catalogs:** Integrate your OPDS server or Calibre library from the settings tab to browse and download books directly inside Yomi.

### 2. Customizing Your Reading Layout
*   While reading a book, tap/click the **Aa** settings menu on the top-right toolbar.
*   Adjust font size, line spacing, font family, and margins to find what is most comfortable for your eyes.
*   Toggle between **Pagination** (book-like page turning) and **Scroll** mode.

### 3. Annotating and Reviewing
*   Highlight any text to instantly save a quote, write a note, or color-code key passages.
*   Access the **Notes & Annotations** sidebar to search through, edit, or export your reading logs.

### 4. Setting up Sync
*   Enable sync in Settings to link your devices. Once set up, your reading progress, notes, and highlights will update in real-time across all your desktop, mobile, and web clients.
